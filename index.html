04

The useful question

“What am I trying not to feel by not starting?”

Boredom? Confusion? Fear of doing it badly? The discomfort of not knowing where to begin? Once you identify the friction, you can change the task instead of demanding more willpower.

05

Why this model works

A compact research layer behind the tool.

Immediate rewards win.

Temporal motivation research explains why distant rewards can lose against smaller rewards available now.

Task aversion matters.

Research identifies task aversiveness, delay, self-efficacy, impulsiveness, and self-control as important correlates of procrastination.

Delay can repair mood — briefly.

Emotion-regulation research describes procrastination as a way of escaping an unpleasant present feeling, even when the future cost is known.
